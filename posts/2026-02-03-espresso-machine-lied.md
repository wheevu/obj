---
title: The morning the espresso machine lied
date: 2026-02-03
tags: coffee, life, can-tho
---

The machine claimed "ready" on its little red diode.
It was not ready.
The first pull came out pale and apologetic; the second seemed personally offended.
I no longer trust indicator lights before the third quiet tick of the boiler.

Coffee here is taken seriously and prepared quickly, usually by someone who would rather you stop watching.
I watched anyway.
The result was a lesson in patience disguised as a beverage.
