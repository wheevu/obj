---
title: I argued with a gecko
date: 2026-03-21
tags: life, can-tho, wildlife
---

There is a gecko living above the bathroom light.
Tonight it made a sound roughly translating to *"you again."*
I told it the ceiling was my territory too.
It blinked slowly, the way a landlord blinks at a late rent complaint, and resumed its post.

We have reached an uneasy détente.
It gets the upper third of the room; I get the lower two-thirds and the mild, ongoing suspicion that it is winning.
